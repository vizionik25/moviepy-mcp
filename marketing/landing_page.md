## Key Features

- **Dual-Interface Flexibility:** Access tools via standard REST endpoints or empower AI agents with our FastMCP server.
- **Pro-Grade Editing Suite:** From basic cuts and crops to advanced effects like Chroma Key, Scene Detection, and Kaleidoscope.
- **Agent-Native Design:** Built specifically to be consumed by LLMs, allowing agents to "see" and "manipulate" video content.
- **Modern Tech Stack:** Powered by FastAPI, MoviePy v2.2, and `uv` for lightning-fast performance and dependency management.

## Technical Advantages

- **High Performance:** Asynchronous endpoints and thread-pooling for heavy video operations.
- **Production-Ready:** Fully containerized with Docker and documented with Swagger UI.
- **Extensible Architecture:** Modular router-based design makes adding new MoviePy filters a breeze.
- **Seamless Frontend:** Includes a Next.js/React management console with an integrated MCP client.

## Use Cases

- **AI Content Creators:** Automate the generation of social media clips from raw footage using LLMs.
- **Video-as-a-Service:** Build the backend for your next SaaS without worrying about low-level video processing.
- **Interactive AI Assistants:** Give your AI agents the ability to create visual explanations or tutorials in real-time.
